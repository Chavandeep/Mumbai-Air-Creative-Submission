# Demonstration script (about 4 minutes)

**0:00 – Overview.** "Most air-quality apps show a number. This one puts the number back into the city." Point at the four figures: 48,381 measured hours; 63% of variation is shared by all stations at once. Show the trend: every monsoon the air clears.

**0:35 – Explore the city.** The built-form grid, streets by carriageway width, rail. Toggle *BEST bus stops* and *Open space: now vs planned*. Drag the period slider to a winter window and watch station colours change. Click Shivaji Nagar; open *Surroundings* and switch the buffer from 500 m to 2 km.

**1:15 – 3D Mumbai.** Around Byculla: 300,000 real footprints raised by BMC's recorded storeys, coloured by use. Switch to *Citywide built-form grid*. "Height is urban form, not pollution — and grey flat blocks are buildings with no recorded storeys; we did not guess."

**1:45 – Understand the pollution.** Hour × month heatmap for Shivaji Nagar: the November–December band. Scroll to "Does the neighbourhood explain the reading?": 25 stations, nine indicators, four radii — no clear association, and the app says so. Then the two inventories: road dust is 72% of total PM but 15% of PM2.5.

**2:30 – Urban equity.** Map *Homes within 500 m of a park*. Compare M/East, H/West and R/Central on the rank plot. Scroll to the Gini chart.

**3:00 – Reimagine Mumbai.** Pick *Combined – NEERI-IITB long-term plan targets*: emissions fall 33% on one inventory and 48% on the other — "the gap is the point". Open *Build the planned parks*, drag to 100%: park access goes from 85% to 98% using the city's own plan. *Extend bus coverage*: 40 stops placed where unserved homes are densest.

**3:40 – What next?** Eight interventions with evidence, limits and the data needed. Move the weight sliders and the top five reorders. Close on *Measure where the questions are*.

**Closing line.** "Everything measured is measured, everything derived is labelled, and nothing here pretends to forecast Mumbai's air."
